## *Here's How to Participate*

####   
***Select a category***

Step 1

Enter your recipe in one of five categories:  

*   Appetizers & Soups
*   Mains
*   Sides
*   Savoury
*   Desserts
*   Breads

​

#### ***Use at least one Sunnah ingredient***

Step 2

**You must use** at least one Sunnah ingredient from the list below:

​

*   Dates
*   Milk
*   Fig
*   Honey
*   Melon
*   Vinegar
*   Olive Oil
*   Mushroom
*   Grapes
*   Pomegranate
*   Barley

#### ***Include a story about the recipe***

Step 3

Cook up a family secret recipe

Or a lockdown success

Mama's handmade dessert

Or a childhood favourite

​

  
Share with us your unique story behind the chosen recipe such as where the recipe originated or why you created it or why you (or your family) loves it.

#### ***Send us a picture***

Step 4

Showcase your photography skills and send us an amazing picture of the dish you've prepared. Entries will be judged on your food presentation skills and visual appeal.

#### ***Tag us on social media***

Step 5

Post your recipe on Instagram (or Facebook) and tag @arriqaaq and #arriqaaqwinterchefcontest.

#### ***Prize Money***

Submit before January 3, 2021

*   1st Prize: Rs 3000
*   2nd Prize: Rs 2000
*   3rd Prize: Rs 1000

​

Winning recipes (and other notable recipes) will be published in [our first issue of](https://www.magazine.arriqaaq.com/) [Arriqaaq Magazine](https://www.magazine.arriqaaq.com/).

#### ***Judging***

Submit before January 3, 2021

*   Entries will be judged on taste, creativity, best use of our product, ease of preparation, and visual appeal.
*   We will test and select a best recipe from each category, then select **THREE** prize winners.
*   Deadline is January 3, 2021.
*   Judging takes place from January 4-8, 2021.
*   Winners will be notified by January 10, 2021 and winning recipes will be published in our first issue of Arriqaaq Magazine.

#### ***Rules***

Submit before January 3, 2021

*   Each and every entry submitted must be the original work of the entrant to be considered a qualified entry.
*   Any entry that has been previously published in any type of material, will be automatically disqualified.
*   Each submitted recipe must not infringe on the copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity or other intellectual property rights of any other person or entity.
*   Each entrant shall acknowledge that other entrants may have similar recipes.
