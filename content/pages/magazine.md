## 🌺 ***What Is Bloom?***

  
**Bloom** is a Magazine by Arriqaaq to empower the hidden gems in our community by providing a platform where they can showcase their creativity.​

When a flower blossoms, its true beauty is there for all to see; similarly when we as human beings, work through odds and strive to go about our lives with ihsaan, we "**bloom**" into our true potential - in all facets of our inner and outer personality.

Essentially,  this is what our magazine aspires to give a peek into - the beauty of life itself, from a Muslim's perspective. In shaa Allah.

* * *

## ❤️ ***Who is it for?***

  
If you're someone who loves to inspire and be inspired, hear and be heard, share your story, learn from others' stories, laugh over life's little anecdotes, cry in dua for yourself and others, connect with those whom you know and don't know yet..  then yes! This is for you and others like you.

* * *

## 🌻 ***Who are we?***

  
We are **YOU** - The voices  within our community. We are a group of talented writers, artists, poets, designers, illustrators, but most importantly we are Muslim students of knowledge, hoping to sprinkle the world with hope (in Allah), love (of Allah), and stay true to the basic principle of our deen - "advise each other to truth and advise each other to patience".
