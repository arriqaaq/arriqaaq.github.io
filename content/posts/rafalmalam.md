This course by **Ustadha Umm Muawiyah** is an explanation of the book of Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah, that teaches us how to understand and respect the great scholars of the past, even when their rulings might seem difficult to comprehend at first glance. In these times of division within our ummah, this course will help you develop wisdom, unity, and a deeper appreciation for our rich Islamic scholarly tradition. The course will be very practical with lots of examples. It will also discuss Usool Al-Hadeeth (foundations of Hadeeth) to some degree.

---

## 📕 **About the book:**

***Raf'u Al-Malam 'An Al-A'immah Al-A'lam*** (Exonerating the Great Imams from Blame) is a remarkable work authored by Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah. It serves the purpose of dispelling any negative perceptions that people may hold about scholars while also shedding light on the factors that have contributed to their disagreements and variances on various Islamic matters, including Fiqh. 

Ultimately, its objective is to instil a deep sense of love and respect for these scholars in our hearts, recognising their invaluable service to the Ummah.

---

## 📖 **What you will learn:**

- A comprehensive study of Shaykh Ibn Taimiyah's book "Raf Al-Malam An Aimmaril Alam"
- How to understand the reasoning behind scholarly rulings that may seem puzzling at first
- The wisdom and context behind why great scholars gave the rulings they did
- Discussion on Usool Al-Hadeeth
- How to approach differences of opinion among scholars with wisdom and respect
- Giving scholars the benefit of the doubt and seeking understanding before criticism
