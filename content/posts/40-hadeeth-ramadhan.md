The new course on the book ***40 hadeeth on Ramadhan*** by Shaikh Saad bin Syed Ash-Shaal is a short 4 week online course for muslim sisters. This course explains the virtues and etiquettes of Ramadhan. The course is taught by our well versed Islamic teacher, **Ustadha** **Umm Muawiyah**.

---

## 📖 **What you will learn:**

1. The Virtues and Etiquettes of Ramadhan  
2. Detailed explanation of 40 hadiths on the topic  
3. Explanation of the book by Shaikh Saad bin Syed Ash-Shaal  
4. Spiritually prepare you for Ramadhan

---

## 🖊️ **Who is this course for?**

- For all Sisters
- Non Muslims sisters can also join

## ✍️ **About the Author**

Shaikh Saad bin Syed Ash-Shaal was born in Egypt in 1963 to a religious family.  
  
He is one of the people of knowledge in the United Arab Emirates and has lived there for a few decades.  
  
He studied under a number of scholars including Shaikh Hassan Saeed, Abu Umar As-Salik Al-Mauritanee and some students of Sheikh Al-Albani to name a few.  
  
He also visited Shaikh Ibn Baz and Shaikh Al-Albani and met Shaikh Ibn Uthaimeen as well. He also attended some lessons of Shaikh Abdul Muhsin Al-Abbad.  
  
He was known as the Imam of Ibrahim Al-Khalil Masjid and Masjid Aisha Umm Al-Mumineen.  
  
He has been teaching for many years and has authored multiple books.  
  
Umm Muawiyah studied multiple books under him and she has also been going to his masjid since Ramadan 2003.
