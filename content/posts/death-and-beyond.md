The new course on ***DEATH AND BEYOND*** by **Ustadha Umm Muawiyah** is a 8-week free online course for sisters. Explore the three questions after death, the grave's experiences, and the events of the Last Day. Delve into deeds leading to Hell and those guiding us to Paradise. Embark on this transformative journey with Ustadha for a profound understanding on this topic.

---

## 📖 **What you will learn:**

- What happens when a person dies including the three questions they will be asked
- What happens in the grave in terms of pleasure and torment
- The various things that will take place on the Last Day eg. the gathering, the accounting, the siraat, the qantarah, etc.
- Hell and the deeds that lead to it
- Paradise and the deeds that lead to it

---

## 🖊️ **Who is this course for?**

- For all Sisters
- Non Muslims ladies can also join

---

## 🎓 **About the Teacher**

Ustadha Umm Muawiyah has sought knowledge for 19.5 years in the UAE, first in English and then in Arabic. Her teachers include Shaikh Azeez Farhan, Shaikh Saad Ash Shaal, Shaikh Ghazi Salem, Shaykh Anees al-Musabi, Shaikh Khalid Ismail, Shaikh Abdullah Al Kamali and Shaikh Umar Saad, all of whom are known as people of knowledge in the UAE.    
  
She has memorised the Quran and has also taught Islamic courses before. She has written articles on her Islamic blogs for many years. She also writes about places to visit in the UAE at her other website. Please visit her beneficial website to find out more.

[https://www.albidaayah.com/](https://www.albidaayah.com/)
