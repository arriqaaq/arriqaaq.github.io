Alḥamdulillāh.

One of the greatest blessings Allah ﷻ bestows upon us is the opportunity to serve His religion by making beneficial knowledge accessible to others. Every book we publish carries that aspiration—that authentic Islamic knowledge reaches more hearts, more homes, and more communities.

Today, by the grace of Allah, we are delighted to announce that **_Blissful Union_** by **Dr. Syed Nooruzuha Barmaver** is now available in **Urdu**.

This translation has been a labour of love, and we pray that Allah ﷻ accepts it from everyone who contributed to its completion.

* * *

## 📖 Why *Blissful Union*?

Marriage is one of the most discussed aspects of life, yet it is often one of the least understood.

Modern discussions are frequently shaped by cultural expectations, social media, or popular psychology, while authentic Islamic guidance is either overlooked or reduced to a handful of legal rulings.

When *Blissful Union* was first published, our intention was to present a balanced, evidence-based guide to marriage and intimacy—one rooted firmly in the Qur'an, authentic Sunnah, and the understanding of our scholars.

By Allah's permission, it has benefited readers from different parts of the world, and we have long hoped to make it accessible to an even wider audience.

* * *

## 🌍 Why Urdu?

For quite some time, we have received requests from readers asking whether *Blissful Union* would be translated into Urdu.

It was a request we deeply appreciated.

Urdu is the language of millions of Muslims, yet comprehensive works discussing marriage and intimacy through the lens of authentic Islamic scholarship remain relatively limited.

We believed that such an important subject should not remain inaccessible simply because of language.

Alḥamdulillāh, that hope has now become a reality.

* * *

## 📚 What You'll Find Inside

The Urdu edition faithfully preserves the content of the original work, covering topics such as:

- Preparing for marriage
- Choosing a righteous spouse
- Building love and companionship
- Rights and responsibilities of husband and wife
- Communication within marriage
- Marital intimacy according to the Qur'an and Sunnah
- Correcting common misconceptions
- Strengthening family life through authentic Islamic guidance

Throughout the book, discussions are supported by evidence from the Qur'an, authentic Hadith, the statements of the scholars, and contemporary research where appropriate.
