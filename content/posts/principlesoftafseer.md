> guard it (from corruption)

Every young Muslim who opens the Qur’an feels the same wonder: *How do I truly understand what I’m reading?* Is tafseer only for great scholars, or is there a path that even a beginner can take with confidence?

This book begins with that question and shines light on the **principles of tafseer**, so that anyone, young or old, student or seeker of knowledge, can begin engaging the Qur’an with understanding. It takes the reader, step by step, through how the Prophet ﷺ explained the Qur’an, how the companions learned, and how scholars after them preserved their insights. It is a **summary of Ibn Taymiyyah’s** ***Muqaddimah fee Usool at-Tafseer***, presented with a modern touch by incorporating contemporary issues in tafsir.

Simple, structured, and rooted in evidences from Quran and authentic Hadith,  this book is for young readers and for anyone, of any age, who wishes to approach the Qur’an with clarity and confidence.

## 🌿 Why should you read this book

- It’s a beginner-friendly introduction to the science of Usul at Tafsir.
- It clears away the myth that tafseer is “locked” for specialists only, and clears up path on how to approach it.
- It shows how the Qur’an itself explains its verses, and how the Prophet ﷺ and his companions lived its meanings.
- It equips readers to spot common errors, avoid opinion-based tafseer, and balance study with personal reflection (tadabbur).

## 💫 How You Will Benefit from this Book

This book is written with a clear purpose: to give you the keys to approach the Qur’an correctly. In a time when personal opinions, sectarian readings, and modernist claims often cloud people’s understanding, this work draws you back to the original sources, the Prophet ﷺ, his Companions, and the earliest scholars.

Here’s what you will gain from it:

- **Clarity on what Tafseer really is.** You will learn the difference between explanation (*tafseer*) and reflection (*tadabbur*), so you don’t confuse heartfelt pondering with scholarly interpretation. Both have their place, but they are not the same.
- **Confidence in authenticity.** By studying how the Prophet ﷺ explained the Qur’an, how the Sahabah carried those explanations, and how the Tābi‘īn preserved them, you’ll see why tafseer is not guesswork but a transmitted science.
- **Awareness of common mistakes.** The book doesn’t just teach what is right it shows what is wrong. Deviant tafsīrs are exposed, so you can recognize false readings and protect yourself from confusion.
- **Understanding of delicate issues.** Topics like *Isra’īliyyāt* (Judeo-Christian reports), “scientific tafseer,” and theories of Qur’anic coherence (*nazm*) are explained with balance: what is useful, what is harmful, and what limits must be respected.
- **Guidance on where to start.** For those who want to continue beyond this summary, the book points you towards authentic tafsīrs like Ibn Kathīr, As-Sa‘di, and Tafseer al-Muyassar — so you know your next steps.

**Note:** This book equips you with the **summary of** **principles of tafseer,** not to make you a mufassir, but to make you a safer reader of the Qur’an. It teaches you how to recognize authentic explanations, avoid misleading ones, and approach the Qur’an with both heart and discipline.
