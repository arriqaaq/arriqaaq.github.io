## ✔️ **Program**

The program duration is 6 weeks.  You'll learn Bash Scripting, Linux, Containers, and more.

1.  Bash Scripting
2.  Linux Kernel
3.  Linux User and Group Management
4.  Linux Networking
5.  Linux Capacity Planning
6.  Containers (Docker and Podman)

* * *

## ⛳ **Why this course?**

The Bootcamp aims to teach you practical linux administration.

* * *

## ⏳ **When Does The Bootcamp Start?**

The Bootcamp starts 18 **June, 2021.** The Bootcamp, is completely online, so you can study during the weekends, alongside your other commitments. That said, we recommend that you practice as often as possible to give yourself the best chance of progressing.
