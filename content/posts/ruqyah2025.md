The new course on **RUQYAH** by our Ustadha Umm Muawiyah is a **FREE** online course for sisters. Learn to recognize signs of magic, jinn possession, evil eye, or envy, and discover how to protect yourself through ruqyah. Understand the wisdom of prophetic medicine, natural Islamic remedies, and understand when to seek both spiritual and medical support. Join us on this journey to strengthen your connection with Allah.

---

## 📖 **What you will learn:**

- How to recognize if you may be experiencing effects of magic, jinn possession, evil eye, or envy
- How to perform ruqyah for yourself and your family
- Various beneficial Islamic remedies including cupping therapy
- How to navigate trials and depression with Islamic guidance and wisdom
- When to seek spiritual remedies versus when to seek medical support

---

## 🖊️ **Who is this course for?**

- All sisters seeking to learn about Islamic healing
- Sisters who want to protect themselves and their families
- Those curious about prophetic medicine and natural remedies
- Non Muslims ladies can also join

## 🎓 **About the Teacher**

Ustadha Umm Muawiyah has sought knowledge for 21 years in the UAE, first in English and then in Arabic. Her teachers include Shaikh Azeez Farhan, Shaikh Saad Ash Shaal, Shaikh Ghazi Salem, Shaykh Anees al-Musabi, Shaikh Khalid Ismail, Shaikh Abdullah Al Kamali and Shaikh Umar Saad, all of whom are known as people of knowledge in the UAE.    
  
She has memorised the Quran and has also taught Islamic courses before. She has written articles on her Islamic blogs for many years. She also writes about places to visit in the UAE at her other website. Please visit her beneficial website to find out more.
