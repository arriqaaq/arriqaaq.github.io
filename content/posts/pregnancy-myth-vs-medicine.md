## 🌼 **About The Theme**

> *“And We have enjoined on man (to be dutiful and good) to his parents. His mother bore him in weakness and hardship upon weakness and hardship, and his weaning is in two years give thanks to Me and to your parents, unto Me is the final destination."  \[Quran – 31:14\]*

  
A journey unlike any other, hardship upon hardship as Allah Himself describes in the Qur'an, yet it is one that takes us on a tumultuous wave of emotions, from amazement to gratitude, from happiness to fear, from feeling like the most important person in the world to feeling completely lonely, with the only constant anchor in our life being the Creator of life Himself - Allah. ***Yes, this is motherhood - it is raw, inspiring, unique and rewarding.***

Join us in this series of podcasts, to shed light upon some of the best practices pertaining to motherhood and childbirth, birth plans, choosing the right health care provider, and busting common myths surrounding pregnancy and newborn care, ان شاء الله.

Keep an eye out for some info packed articles on Pregnancy & Postpartum Nutrition, exclusively on [ARRIQAAQ HEALTH](/tag/health/)!
