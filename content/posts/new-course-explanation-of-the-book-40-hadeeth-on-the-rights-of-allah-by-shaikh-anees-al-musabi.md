The new course on the book ***40 hadeeth on the Rights of Allah*** by Shaikh Anees Al-Musabi is a short 6 week course for muslim sisters taught online. This course is important to help understand the rights of Allah, our Rabb. The course is taught by the well versed Islamic teacher, **Ustadha** **Umm Muawiyah**.

---

## 📖 **What you will learn:**

1. The Rights of Allah  
2. Detailed explanation of 40 hadiths on the topic  
3. Explanation of the book by Shaikh Anees Al-Musabi

---

## 🖊️ **Who is this course for?**

-  Sisters only

---

## 🎓 **About the Teacher**

Ustadha Umm Muawiyah has sought knowledge for 18.5 years in the UAE, first in English and then in Arabic. Her teachers include Shaikh Azeez Farhan, Shaikh Saad Ash Shaal, Shaikh Ghazi Salem, Shaykh Anees al-Musabi, Shaikh Khalid Ismail, Shaikh Abdullah Al Kamali and Shaikh Umar Saad, all of whom are known as people of knowledge in the UAE.   
  
She has memorised the Quran and has also taught Islamic courses before. She has written articles on her Islamic blogs for many years. She also writes about places to visit in the UAE at her other website. 

---

## ✍️ **About the Author**

Shaikh Anees bin Nasir Al-Musabi is one of the people of knowledge in the United Arab Emirates.  
  
He learnt under multiple scholars including Shaikh Abdul Bari Al-Fath (with whom he studied Sahih Al Bukhari and Sahih Muslim), Shaikh Hamad Al-Aqeel (with whom he studied Buloogh Al-Maram), and Shaikh Saad Ash-Shalwi. He also attended the classes of Shaikh Ibn Baz and Shaikh Uthaimeen.  
  
He also teaches various courses. Umm Muawiyah studied multiple books of Aqeedah under him as well as the Fiqh of Zakah and Marriage and Divorce.
