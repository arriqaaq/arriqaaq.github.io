We are so excited to announce the launch of our brand-new book, "My First Hajj - A Sacred Journey," along with its exciting companion activity book. ## 🎨 About the Book

As a parent, we all cherish the desire to nurture a deep love for Islam in our children's hearts. We want them to understand the beauty and significance of our faith, to feel connected to our rich history and traditions, and to grow up to be kind, compassionate, and God-fearing individuals. And what better way to do that than by introducing them to one of the most important pillars of Islam – the sacred pilgrimage of Hajj.

We understand that explaining the intricacies of Hajj to young children can be a daunting task. That's why we've created this delightful book to make learning about Hajj a joyous and engaging experience for kids of all ages.

Through vibrant illustrations, playful storytelling, and a warm, friendly tone, this book takes our children on an exciting virtual tour of Mecca, guiding them through every step of the Hajj journey. From putting on the special Ihram clothes to circling the Kaaba, through important moments like reflection and prayer at Mount Arafat and Udhiyyah, this book makes each step of the pilgrimage fun and easy to understand. Your kids will love learning how Hajj is performed and discovering the beauty of this sacred adventure.

## 🖌️ **How To Use This Book**

1. Print the pdf out on white A3/A4 paper
2. Bind it at your local stationery shop
3. Remember to set your printer for ‘borderless’ and ‘two sided’ for binding.
4. Allow your child to use colourful marker pens or scissors to colour, cut and stick the stickers as they wish.
