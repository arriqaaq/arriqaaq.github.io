Join us for the Ramadhan workshop on "***Getting the Most out of the Greatest Month***" by **Ustadhah Umm Muawiyah**! This 2-part free online workshop is exclusively for sisters and will provide a comprehensive understanding of Ramadan's importance and offer practical guidance on maximizing its blessings. Don't miss this opportunity to prepare for Ramadhan!

---

## 🖊️ **What will you learn?**

- Some of the virtues of Ramadan  
- ⁠Some of the virtues of fasting  
- ⁠The important acts of worship to focus on during Ramadan  
- ⁠How to improve in the above mentioned acts of worship  
- ⁠Some important rulings related to Ramadan

---

## 🖊️ **Who is this workshop for?**

- For all Sisters
- Non Muslims ladies can also join

---

## 🎓 **About the Teacher**

Ustadhah Umm Muawiyah has sought knowledge for 19.5 years in the UAE, first in English and then in Arabic. Her teachers include Shaikh Azeez Farhan, Shaikh Saad Ash Shaal, Shaikh Ghazi Salem, Shaykh Anees al-Musabi, Shaikh Khalid Ismail, Shaikh Abdullah Al Kamali and Shaikh Umar Saad, all of whom are known as people of knowledge in the UAE.    
  
She has memorised the Quran and has also taught Islamic courses before. She has written articles on her Islamic blogs for many years. She also writes about places to visit in the UAE at her other website. Please visit her beneficial website to find out more.

[https://www.albidaayah.com/](https://www.albidaayah.com/)
