The **ARABIC LEARNER** Series is a short Ramadan Arabic course for young Arabic explorers. This course is ideal for the mother and the child to attend together, taught by our well versed Arabic instructor, **Ustadha** **Zahra Jukaku** who is a graduate in Sharee’ah from University of Sharjah.

* * *

## 📖 **What you will learn:**

1\. Ramadan related vocabulary in Arabic  
2\. Numbers up to 10  
3\. One Color a week  
4\. Learn related Duas  
5\. Activities: Story telling, rhymes

Skills learnt at the end of the course: Basic Arabic reading

* * *

## 🖊️ **Who is this course for?**

*   Kids in the age group 4-6 (accompanied by the mother)
*   Muslim mothers

* * *

## 🎓 **About the Teacher**

Ustadha Zahra Jukaku is a graduate in Sharee’ah from University of Sharjah and has taken many classes for young students interested in Arabic.

* * *
